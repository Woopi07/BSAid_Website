Our Services

Keeping your IT infrastructure at its best

With data center technical expertise, professional consulting, and dependable maintenance agreements, PTSI helps businesses stay secure, efficient, and ahead of the curve.

Data Center Technical Services

PTSI’s Data Center Technical Services deliver complete solutions to optimize, secure, and manage your IT infrastructure, ensuring reliable performance and business continuity.

  • Converged and Hyper-converged Infrastructure

    Design, deployment and lifecycle support for converged and hyper-converged platforms such as Dell VxRail, Nutanix and HPE SimpliVity, combining compute, storage and networking in one system.

  • Virtualization

    Planning, installation and optimization of VMware, Microsoft Hyper-V and other hypervisors to consolidate servers and make better use of your hardware.

  • Servers

    Rack, tower and blade server sizing, installation, configuration, firmware updates and health checks.

  • Storage

    SAN, NAS and all-flash storage design, deployment, expansion and performance tuning.

  • Backup and Replication

    Backup, replication and disaster-recovery setup with tools such as Veeam, Veritas, Commvault and Acronis, including regular restore testing.

  • Networking and Cybersecurity

    LAN, WAN, wireless and firewall deployment, plus security hardening to protect your infrastructure.

  • Network Monitoring and Management

    Continuous monitoring of network devices and links, with alerts and reports that help catch issues before they cause downtime.

  • IT Asset and Management

    Inventory, tracking and lifecycle management of hardware and software assets, including licenses and warranties.

  • Endpoint Management

    Central deployment, patching and policy control for desktops, laptops and mobile devices.

  • Unified Threat Management

    Setup and tuning of all-in-one security appliances that combine firewall, intrusion prevention, antivirus, web filtering and VPN.

  • Vulnerability Assessment

    Scanning and analysis of your systems and network to find security weaknesses, with prioritized recommendations to fix them.

Professional Services

Our team of experts offers strategic guidance, implementation support, and ongoing management to ensure seamless IT performance.

  • Project Management

    A dedicated project manager plans scope, schedule and resources, and coordinates every rollout through to handover.

  • Data Migration

    Secure transfer of data, applications and workloads between systems, storage or data centers with minimal downtime.

  • IT Outsourcing

    Skilled IT personnel assigned on-site or remotely to support your operations and extend your in-house team.

  • Audiovisual

    Boardroom, training-room and conferencing AV: displays, projectors, audio and video-conferencing systems.

  • Structured Cabling

    Standards-compliant copper and fiber cabling, racks and patching, tested and documented for reliable connectivity.

  • Physical Security and Surveillance technology

    CCTV, access control and premises-protection systems designed and installed to secure your facilities.

  • Data Center fit out

    Design and build of server rooms and data centers, including racks, raised floors, containment and cooling.

  • Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, Fire Protection, and Sanitary

    MEPFS works that power, cool and protect your data center and facilities, coordinated with the rest of the build.

Maintenance Agreement

Our service options include 8x5 and 24/7 support, giving you the flexibility to choose the coverage that best fits your business needs.

  • Customizable SLA

    Service levels tailored to your business, with response and resolution times that match how critical each system is.

  • Cost-Effective Support

    Predictable maintenance costs, often lower than renewing manufacturer support or replacing equipment early.

  • On-site Support

    Engineers dispatched to your location for hands-on troubleshooting, repairs and replacements.

  • Remote Support

    Fast remote diagnosis and fixes, so many issues are resolved without waiting for a site visit.

  • Phone and Email Support

    Direct access to our technical team by phone and email for questions, incidents and service requests.

  • Dedicated Spare Units

    Spare parts and units kept on standby to keep you running while faulty equipment is repaired.

  • RMA / Technical Support & Assistance

    We handle manufacturer return-merchandise authorizations and warranty claims on your behalf.

  • IT Asset and Management

    Records of covered equipment, contract dates and service history so nothing falls out of coverage.
